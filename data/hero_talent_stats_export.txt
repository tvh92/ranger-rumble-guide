RANGER RUMBLE HERO TALENT EXPORT
Source: version 1.10.3.149 APK HeroSpec TalentsInUnlockOrder and referenced gameplay records
Fixed-point divisor: 65536
Damage arrays: levels 1 through 10
Unlock requirements: Talent 1 unlocks at level 3 and costs 1 core. Talent 2 unlocks at level 6, costs 3 cores, and requires Talent 1 to be unlocked first.
