OUTDATED TALENTS AND WEAPON MODS

Historical reference from an older Ranger Rumble build. These names and descriptions may not match the current game.

TALENTS

Celeste
- Turbo Dash: Celeste's dash recharges faster.
- Improved Melee: Unlocks a new jump melee attack. Also, the melee weapon provides more ultimate charge.

Chip
- Elemental Scholar: Chip electrocutes and freezes other rangers faster.
- Improved Melee: Unlocks a new jump melee attack. Also, the melee weapon uses the damage type of the currently equipped weapon.

Sparky
- Fire Affinity: Sparky's fire damage is more effective.
- Improved Melee: Unlocks a new jump melee attack. Also, jump attacks deal fire damage in an area on the ground.

Grimshot
- Scope Master: Snipers have more bullets and reload faster when Grimshot uses them.
- Improved Melee: Unlocks a new jump melee attack. Also, the melee weapon grants a short speed bonus on the third hit.

Lil' Ann
- Adrenaline Spike: Knocking out another ranger grants Lil'Ann a temporary speed bonus.
- Improved Melee: Unlocks a new jump melee attack. Also, the melee weapon deals more damage to targets with low health.

Lump
- Evolving Experiment: Lump is made of Acid and can evolve through his ultimate ability.
- Improved Melee: Unlocks a new jump melee attack. Also, jump attacks create acid puddles on the ground.

Tempest
- Quiet Strength: Instead of the usual regeneration, Tempest regenerates slowly and continuously over time.
- Improved Melee: Unlocks a new jump melee attack. Also, the melee weapon deals more damage to targets with low health.

Mopz
- Electric Affinity: Mopz's electric damage is more effective.
- Improved Melee: Unlocks a new jump melee attack. Also, the melee weapon deals electric damage.

Widget
- Express Recovery: Widget regenerates faster than other heroes.
- Improved Melee: Unlocks a new jump melee attack. Also, jump attacks push enemies away.

Sprocket
- Rocket Legs: Sprocket jumps higher from the ground.
- Improved Melee: Unlocks a new jump melee attack. Also, jump attacks attract enemies.

Zed One
- Artillery Ace: Zed One is not slowed down when firing his weapons.
- Improved Melee: Unlocks a new jump melee attack. Also, the melee weapon provides ammo or speeds up reloading if it is in progress.

WEAPON MODS

WEAPON MODS

Amoeboid Launcher
- Acid Spill: Small allied Amoeboids now spawn an acid puddle on death.
- Brainwash: Shoot the neutral Amoeboids on the map to recruit them.
- Primal Goo: The first shot after reloading immediately spawns a big Amoeboid.

Burst Pistol
- Lightweight Frame: Wielding the Burst Pistol grants a speed bonus.
- Double Barrel: Each shot now fires two projectiles instead of one.
- Triple Barrel: Each shot now fires three projectiles.

Bombardier
- Payload Rack: The Bombardier drops more bombs and moves faster.
- Seeking Payload: The Bombardier drops even more bombs and slightly turns toward nearby enemies.
- Crash Core: The Bombardier drops to the ground and explodes at the end of its flight.

Buzz Blades
- Ricochet Blades: The number of rebounds from the blades increases.
- Splitting Blades: Blades split in two after impact.
- Cyclone Bounce: The number of rebounds from the blades increases even more.

Cold Snap
- Cryo Shards: Projectiles leave ice shards on the ground after exploding.
- Frost Disperser: Projectiles now also leave an icy cloud in the air after exploding, freezing other rivals over time.
- Shard Cascade: An additional circle of ice shards appears following the first one.

Cryo Slider
- Cryo Stone: The stone now explodes into an area of ice damage.
- Bounce Coating: After bouncing, the stone accelerates and becomes more powerful.
- Splitter Stone: After each bounce, the stone divides into two.

The Enforcer
- Tri Chamber: The number of shots per burst increases to 3.
- Shock Round: Shots inflict more damage on electrocuted rivals.
- Quad Chamber: The number of shots per burst increases to 4.

Blackhole Storm
- Spin-Up Rotor: The rate of fire can increase even further.
- Quick Reload: When reloading, a quick reload button appears to be pressed at the right timing.
- Storm Module: After holding fire for a sufficient duration, consume all remaining ammo to unleash a wide laser beam.

Glove of Doom
- Extra Agent: Agents of doom are now generated two by two.
- Jetpack Unit: Agents of doom now use a jetpack to fly towards nearby enemies.
- Agent Alpha: Grants an extra ammo at the end of the magazine that generates an Alpha Agent.

Shatterbomb
- Cluster Bombs: Grenades release two bomblets after exploding.
- Shardfield Core: Grenades leave shards on the ground, dealing damage over time.
- Mega Cluster: Grenades now release four bomblets after exploding.

Headhunter
- Thermal Scope: Rivals are displayed through walls at the center of the reticle.
- Enhanced Optics: The Headhunter can now use a zoom lens.
- Sharpshot Ammo: Zooming shots inflict more damage.

Holoshield Glove
- Healing Field: Shields now heal allies directly behind them.
- Phalanx: The Holoshield Glove can now deploy more simultaneous shields.
- Holoshock Module: Shields deal electrical damage on contact and immediately stun.

Hoverboots
- Impact Thruster: Hoverboots now deal more damage on contact.
- Hoversocks: Using the Hoverboots grants a temporary health bonus.
- Shockwave Starter: Create an area of damage around the player when Hoverboots are equipped.

Lava Gun
- Magma Nozzle: Firing leaves lava on the ground, burning enemies standing on it.
- Wideflow Canon: The lava stream is now wider.
- Viscous Lava: The lava becomes more powerful and slows enemies down.

Hunter Mine Launcher
- Mine Fusion: Shooting on an existing mine allows them to be merged into a larger one.
- Seeker Mines: When a mine detonates, it now moves towards nearby enemies.
- Stealth Casing: Mines become camouflaged and are harder to spot.

Plasma Striker
- Multi Capacitor: One additional beam can be fired per shot, by charging longer.
- Lance Loader: One more beam can be fired per shot, without additional charging time.
- Overdrive Tip: When fully charged, all beams cause a small explosion upon impact.

Pyrocitor
- Azur Igniter: After holding fire for a sufficient duration, the flame turns blue and inflicts more damage.
- Oil Canister: When reloading, shoots oil puddles that can ignite with fire.
- Reservoir: Oil puddles produced by the Pyrocitor are now larger.

Blitz Gun
- Impact Chamber: Firing shortly after a dash delivers a more powerful shot.
- Kinetic Loader: The weapon recovers ammo after a dash.
- Overdrive Coil: When having half your health or less, the Blitz Gun deals more damage.

Tesla Claw
- Dual Arc Emitter: The Tesla Claw can now hit two enemies at the same time.
- Conductive Antena: After holding fire for a sufficient duration, the beam becomes larger and more powerful.
- Tri Arc Emitter: The Tesla Claw can now hit three enemies at the same time.

Void Repulser
- Pulse Core: When the Void Repulser reloads, it deals damage in an area in front of it.
- Shatter Booster: Grants a short speed bonus if the Void Repulser runs out of health.
- Ramming Field: After being held for a duration, the Void Repulser increases the hero's speed and deals more damage on contact.

Warmonger
- Rocket Cluster: Fires two minirockets in addition to the usual one.
- Extra Rocket: An additional minirocket is fired alongside the others.
- Incendiary Rockets: Minirockets now deal fire damage.
