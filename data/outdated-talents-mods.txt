OUTDATED TALENTS AND WEAPON MODS

Historical reference from an older Ranger Rumble build. These names and descriptions may not match the current game.

TALENTS

Celeste
- Turbo Dash: Celeste's dash recharges faster.
- Improved Melee: Unlocks a new jump melee attack. Also, the melee weapon provides more ultimate charge.

Chip
- Elemental Scholar: Chip electrocutes and freezes other rangers faster.
- Improved Melee: Unlocks a new jump melee attack. Also, the melee weapon uses the damage type of the currently equipped weapon.

Sparky
- Fire Affinity: Sparky's fire damage is more effective.
- Improved Melee: Unlocks a new jump melee attack. Also, jump attacks deal fire damage in an area on the ground.

Grimshot
- Scope Master: Snipers have more bullets and reload faster when Grimshot uses them.
- Improved Melee: Unlocks a new jump melee attack. Also, the melee weapon grants a short speed bonus on the third hit.

Lil' Ann
- Adrenaline Spike: Knocking out another ranger grants Lil'Ann a temporary speed bonus.
- Improved Melee: Unlocks a new jump melee attack. Also, the melee weapon deals more damage to targets with low health.

Lump
- Evolving Experiment: Lump is made of Acid and can evolve through his ultimate ability.
- Improved Melee: Unlocks a new jump melee attack. Also, jump attacks create acid puddles on the ground.

Tempest
- Quiet Strength: Instead of the usual regeneration, Tempest regenerates slowly and continuously over time.
- Improved Melee: Unlocks a new jump melee attack. Also, the melee weapon deals more damage to targets with low health.

Mopz
- Electric Affinity: Mopz's electric damage is more effective.
- Improved Melee: Unlocks a new jump melee attack. Also, the melee weapon deals electric damage.

Widget
- Express Recovery: Widget regenerates faster than other heroes.
- Improved Melee: Unlocks a new jump melee attack. Also, jump attacks push enemies away.

Sprocket
- Rocket Legs: Sprocket jumps higher from the ground.
- Improved Melee: Unlocks a new jump melee attack. Also, jump attacks attract enemies.

Zed One
- Artillery Ace: Zed One is not slowed down when firing his weapons.
- Improved Melee: Unlocks a new jump melee attack. Also, the melee weapon provides ammo or speeds up reloading if it is in progress.

WEAPON MODS

Amoeboid Launcher

- Acid Spill (Amoeboid Launcher): Small allied Amoeboids now spawn an acid puddle on death.
- Brainwash (Amoeboid Launcher): Shoot the neutral Amoeboids on the map to recruit them.
- Primal Goo (Amoeboid Launcher): The first shot after reloading immediately spawns a big Amoeboid.
Burst Pistol

- Lightweight Frame (Burst Pistol): Wielding the Burst Pistol grants a speed bonus.
- Double Barrel (Burst Pistol): Each shot now fires two projectiles instead of one.
- Triple Barrel (Burst Pistol): Each shot now fires three projectiles.
Bombardier

- Payload Rack (Bombardier): The Bombardier drops more bombs and moves faster.
- Seeking Payload (Bombardier): The Bombardier drops even more bombs and slightly turns toward nearby enemies.
- Crash Core (Bombardier): The Bombardier drops to the ground and explodes at the end of its flight.
Buzz Blades

- Ricochet Blades (Buzz Blades): The number of rebounds from the blades increases.
- Splitting Blades (Buzz Blades): Blades split in two after impact.
- Cyclone Bounce (Buzz Blades): The number of rebounds from the blades increases even more.
Cold Snap

- Cryo Shards (Cold Snap): Projectiles leave ice shards on the ground after exploding.
- Frost Disperser (Cold Snap): Projectiles now also leave an icy cloud in the air after exploding, freezing other rivals over time.
- Shard Cascade (Cold Snap): An additional circle of ice shards appears following the first one.
Cryo Slider

- Cryo Stone (Cryo Slider): The stone now explodes into an area of ice damage.
- Bounce Coating (Cryo Slider): After bouncing, the stone accelerates and becomes more powerful.
- Splitter Stone (Cryo Slider): After each bounce, the stone divides into two.
The Enforcer

- Tri Chamber (The Enforcer): The number of shots per burst increases to 3.
- Shock Round (The Enforcer): Shots inflict more damage on electrocuted rivals.
- Quad Chamber (The Enforcer): The number of shots per burst increases to 4.
Blackhole Storm

- Spin-Up Rotor (Blackhole Storm): The rate of fire can increase even further.
- Quick Reload (Blackhole Storm): When reloading, a quick reload button appears to be pressed at the right timing.
- Storm Module (Blackhole Storm): After holding fire for a sufficient duration, consume all remaining ammo to unleash a wide laser beam.
Glove of Doom

- Extra Agent (Glove of Doom): Agents of doom are now generated two by two.
- Jetpack Unit (Glove of Doom): Agents of doom now use a jetpack to fly towards nearby enemies.
- Agent Alpha (Glove of Doom): Grants an extra ammo at the end of the magazine that generates an Alpha Agent.
Shatterbomb

- Cluster Bombs (Shatterbomb): Grenades release two bomblets after exploding.
- Shardfield Core (Shatterbomb): Grenades leave shards on the ground, dealing damage over time.
- Mega Cluster (Shatterbomb): Grenades now release four bomblets after exploding.
Headhunter

- Thermal Scope (Headhunter): Rivals are displayed through walls at the center of the reticle.
- Enhanced Optics (Headhunter): The Headhunter can now use a zoom lens.
- Sharpshot Ammo (Headhunter): Zooming shots inflict more damage.
Holoshield Glove

- Healing Field (Holoshield Glove): Shields now heal allies directly behind them.
- Phalanx (Holoshield Glove): The Holoshield Glove can now deploy more simultaneous shields.
- Holoshock Module (Holoshield Glove): Shields deal electrical damage on contact and immediately stun.
Hoverboots

- Impact Thruster (Hoverboots): Hoverboots now deal more damage on contact.
- Hoversocks (Hoverboots): Using the Hoverboots grants a temporary health bonus.
- Shockwave Starter (Hoverboots): Create an area of damage around the player when Hoverboots are equipped.
Lava Gun

- Magma Nozzle (Lava Gun): Firing leaves lava on the ground, burning enemies standing on it.
- Wideflow Canon (Lava Gun): The lava stream is now wider.
- Viscous Lava (Lava Gun): The lava becomes more powerful and slows enemies down.
Hunter Mine Launcher

- Mine Fusion (Hunter Mine Launcher): Shooting on an existing mine allows them to be merged into a larger one.
- Seeker Mines (Hunter Mine Launcher): When a mine detonates, it now moves towards nearby enemies.
- Stealth Casing (Hunter Mine Launcher): Mines become camouflaged and are harder to spot.
Plasma Striker

- Multi Capacitor (Plasma Striker): One additional beam can be fired per shot, by charging longer.
- Lance Loader (Plasma Striker): One more beam can be fired per shot, without additional charging time.
- Overdrive Tip (Plasma Striker): When fully charged, all beams cause a small explosion upon impact.
Pyrocitor

- Azur Igniter (Pyrocitor): After holding fire for a sufficient duration, the flame turns blue and inflicts more damage.
- Oil Canister (Pyrocitor): When reloading, shoots oil puddles that can ignite with fire.
- Reservoir (Pyrocitor): Oil puddles produced by the Pyrocitor are now larger.
Blitz Gun

- Impact Chamber (Blitz Gun): Firing shortly after a dash delivers a more powerful shot.
- Kinetic Loader (Blitz Gun): The weapon recovers ammo after a dash.
- Overdrive Coil (Blitz Gun): When having half your health or less, the Blitz Gun deals more damage.
Tesla Claw

- Dual Arc Emitter (Tesla Claw): The Tesla Claw can now hit two enemies at the same time.
- Conductive Antena (Tesla Claw): After holding fire for a sufficient duration, the beam becomes larger and more powerful.
- Tri Arc Emitter (Tesla Claw): The Tesla Claw can now hit three enemies at the same time.
Void Repulser

- Pulse Core (Void Repulser): When the Void Repulser reloads, it deals damage in an area in front of it.
- Shatter Booster (Void Repulser): Grants a short speed bonus if the Void Repulser runs out of health.
- Ramming Field (Void Repulser): After being held for a duration, the Void Repulser increases the hero's speed and deals more damage on contact.
Warmonger

- Rocket Cluster (Warmonger): Fires two minirockets in addition to the usual one.
- Extra Rocket (Warmonger): An additional minirocket is fired alongside the others.
- Incendiary Rockets (Warmonger): Minirockets now deal fire damage.
